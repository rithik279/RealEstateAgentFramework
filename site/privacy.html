Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 1, 2026

Anu Kabli, licensed REALTOR® with IQI Global Real Estate ("we," "us," or "our"), is committed to protecting your personal privacy. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, and protect the personal information you provide when using this website or engaging our real estate services.

By using this website, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

1. Information We Collect

We collect personal information only as necessary to provide you with real estate services, including:

  • Full name, phone number, and email address
  • Property preferences including budget, desired areas, bedrooms, and timeline
  • Current housing situation and financing status
  • Any other information you voluntarily provide through our contact forms or during consultations

We also collect anonymous, aggregate usage data through cookies and analytics tools (such as Google Analytics) to improve the performance and usability of this website. This data cannot be used to identify you individually.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your personal information is used solely to provide and improve our real estate services, including:

  • Contacting you to discuss your real estate needs and connect you with suitable properties
  • Matching you with homes listed on the MLS® system that meet your stated criteria
  • Connecting you with pre-vetted mortgage brokers, home inspectors, lawyers, and other service providers
  • Sending you relevant market updates, listings, and follow-up communications
  • Complying with legal and regulatory requirements, including those imposed by TRREB and RECO

We do not sell, trade, rent, or exchange your personal information with third parties for any commercial purpose.

3. Consent to Communication

By submitting your contact information through this website, you provide express written consent to be contacted by Anu Kabli and IQI Global Real Estate via phone, email, or SMS regarding your real estate inquiry. These communications may include information about listings, market updates, and our services.

In accordance with Canada's Anti-Spam Legislation (CASL) and the Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA), you may withdraw your consent at any time by:

  • Replying STOP to any SMS message
  • Clicking "unsubscribe" in any email we send you
  • Contacting us directly at the address below

We will process all opt-out requests within 10 business days and send a final confirmation of your opt-out.

4. Information Sharing

We will not disclose your personal information to any third party except in the following circumstances:

  • With the Toronto Regional Real Estate Board (TRREB) for auditing and/or legal compliance purposes
  • With IQI Global Real Estate brokerage as required for transactions and regulatory compliance
  • With service providers directly involved in your transaction (e.g., mortgage broker, lawyer, home inspector) only with your knowledge or at your request
  • When required by law, court order, or lawful authority

We are the sole owner of all information collected on this website. We impose strict access controls on all employees and representatives who may have access to your data.

5. Cookies & Analytics

This website uses the following cookies:

  • Google Analytics: Collects depersonalised usage data (page views, session duration, traffic sources) to help us improve the website. This data cannot be linked back to any individual.
  • Google Ads: Used to understand which pages lead to contact form submissions, helping us improve our advertising effectiveness.

You may disable cookies through your browser settings at any time without affecting your ability to use this website.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. While we strive to protect your data, no method of transmission over the Internet is 100% secure.

7. Access, Correction & Deletion

You have the right to request access to the personal information we hold about you, to request corrections, or to request erasure of your data. To make such a request, please contact us in writing at the address below. We will verify your identity and respond within a reasonable timeframe.

Please note that some information may be required to be retained for regulatory or legal compliance purposes and may not be eligible for immediate deletion.

8. Links to Third-Party Websites

This website may contain links to third-party sites, including real estate listing platforms and brokerage pages. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of those sites. We encourage you to review the privacy policies of any third-party sites you visit.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date. We will make reasonable efforts to notify you directly of significant changes. Continued use of this website after any changes constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.

10. MLS® Data — TRREB

Any MLS® listing data accessible through or referenced by this website is provided under license from the Toronto Regional Real Estate Board (TRREB). Such data is intended only for consumers with a bona fide interest in the purchase, sale, or lease of real estate and may not be used for any commercial purpose. TRREB assumes no responsibility for the accuracy of information shown.
