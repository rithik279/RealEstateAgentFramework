About Mount Pleasant

Mount Pleasant is one of Brampton's most thoughtfully designed communities — a transit-oriented neighbourhood built around the Mount Pleasant GO Station on the Kitchener GO line. Unlike most suburban developments, Mount Pleasant was planned from the ground up to put daily life within walking or cycling distance of transit, schools, parks, and shops. The result is a neighbourhood that feels genuinely complete rather than car-dependent.

Located in northwest Brampton, centred on Creditview Road between Bovaird Drive and Sandalwood Parkway, Mount Pleasant features a mix of freehold townhomes, semi-detached homes, and detached properties built primarily between 2007 and 2018 by several builders including Mattamy Homes, Branthaven, and Daniels. The neighbourhood has a cohesive aesthetic with consistent streetscaping and architectural guidelines that keep the community looking well-maintained.

🚆 Key advantage: Mount Pleasant GO Station sits within the community. Commuters walk from their front door to the train in under 15 minutes. This makes Mount Pleasant one of the few Brampton neighbourhoods where car-free commuting to downtown Toronto is genuinely practical.

Why Mount Pleasant is Perfect for Commuters

  • Mount Pleasant GO Station — On the Kitchener line: Union Station in 50–60 minutes. Park and ride facility, plus easy walking/cycling from most streets in the community
  • ZÜM Bus routes — Multiple Brampton Transit routes connecting throughout the city

Schools in Mount Pleasant

  • Mount Pleasant Village Public School — Within community walking distance, K–8 public school
  • St. Bonaventure Catholic School — Catholic elementary option in the neighbourhood

Frequently Asked Questions

Does Mount Pleasant Brampton have a GO Station?
Yes — Mount Pleasant GO Station is located within the community on the Kitchener GO line. It provides train service to Union Station in approximately 50–60 minutes and includes park and ride facilities.
What are home prices in Mount Pleasant Brampton?
Townhomes start around $680,000, semi-detached homes range $800,000–$950,000, and detached homes are typically $880,000–$1,150,000 depending on size and upgrades.
Is Mount Pleasant Brampton a good place for families?
Yes — Mount Pleasant was designed specifically as a family community with schools, parks, trails, a library, and a recreation centre all built in. The GO Station walkability and neighbourhood completeness make it one of Brampton's most family-friendly communities.
