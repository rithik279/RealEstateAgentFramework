About Castlemore

Castlemore stands apart in Brampton's residential landscape. This northeast Brampton enclave — bounded roughly by Mayfield Road to the north, Airport Road to the east, and the Highway 427/50 corridor to the west — is home to some of the most impressive detached homes in the entire city. Wide tree-lined streets, double and triple-car garages, stone-and-brick exteriors, and homes built to impress: Castlemore is where Brampton's established families put down roots.

The neighbourhood has become a defining community for Brampton's South Asian population, particularly Punjabi families who have built their lives and businesses in the GTA and sought homes that reflect their success. Walking through Castlemore, you'll find immaculately maintained properties, active community gatherings, and a neighbourhood pride that is palpable.

Homes in Castlemore are predominantly built from the late 1990s through the 2010s — large enough for multigenerational living, with spacious basements that often include in-law suites or complete secondary dwelling units. This makes Castlemore particularly attractive to extended families and to buyers who want to offset their mortgage costs with rental income from a basement apartment.

Why Castlemore Stands Out

  • Home sizes — Most Castlemore homes are 2,500–5,000 sq ft, significantly larger than comparable-priced properties in Mississauga or Vaughan
  • Multigenerational living — Spacious layouts with walkout or finished basements ideally suited for in-law suites — common among South Asian families keeping extended family close
  • Cardinal Ambrozic Catholic Secondary School — One of the most sought-after secondary schools in Brampton, consistently performing above provincial averages
  • Highway access — Highway 427/50 corridor provides direct access to Pearson Airport (15 min), Vaughan (20 min), and downtown Toronto (45 min without traffic)
  • Strong community — Active gurdwaras, temples, and community organizations within minutes of Castlemore
  • Investment stability — Castlemore properties hold value well even in softer markets because supply is limited and demand from established families remains consistent
