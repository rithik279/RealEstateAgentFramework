The Process

8 Steps. No Surprises. No Runaround.

Most buyers feel lost after their first offer falls through. This process makes sure that never happens to you.

01
You Fill Out the Form

Takes about 2 minutes. Tell me your budget, what neighbourhoods you like, and when you want to move. That's it.

Day 0
02
I Call You Within 5 Minutes

A quick call so I can hear exactly what you need. No scripts, no pressure. Just a real conversation in your language.

Within 5 min
03
3 Homes Picked for You

Same day, I send you 3 homes matched to what you told me. Each one comes with a note explaining exactly why it fits your situation.

Within 1 hour
04
We Tour All 3 in One Day

We see every home together. At each one, I walk you through what it's actually worth, what I like about it, and what to watch out for.

Day 1 to 2
05
We Pick Your Favourite

After the tours, we talk through your top choice. I give you my honest opinion and help you decide with full confidence, not guesswork.

Day 2
06
Your Custom Offer Strategy

I pull 90 days of sold data for that street, factor in the home's condition, and build you a written offer strategy. You see the exact number, the reasoning behind it, and what leverage you have before you sign anything.

Day 2 to 3
07
I Negotiate. You Stay Calm.

I handle every counter, every clause, every back-and-forth. Inspectors, lawyers, your mortgage broker — all coordinated by me. You get one update at each step, in plain language, with zero paperwork confusion.

Day 3 to 5
08
Keys in Your Hand

You close and get your keys. I don't disappear after that. I'm available for questions, market updates, and whatever comes next.
