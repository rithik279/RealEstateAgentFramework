What Is Rosedale Village?

★ Age-Restricted Community — 55+ Residents Only

Rosedale Village is a private, gated adult lifestyle community in northwest Brampton — specifically at the intersection of Sandalwood Parkway and Highway 410. Developed by Marycroft Homes, it is consistently described as the GTA's premier 55+ community.

The neighbourhood is built around an executive par-3 9-hole golf course, with a full clubhouse serving as the hub for social, recreational, and fitness activity. 24-hour automated gated security and professional landscaping management mean residents live without the exterior maintenance burdens of traditional homeownership.

This is where Brampton's retirees, semi-retirees, and empty nesters land after selling family homes — often coming from Castlemore, Credit Valley, or Mississauga. The address keeps them near adult children and grandchildren in Brampton while dramatically reducing day-to-day maintenance and increasing social connection.

The Golf Course

Rosedale Village's executive par-3 9-hole golf course is the neighbourhood's centerpiece. It is designed to be accessible to both experienced golfers and complete beginners:

  • Executive par-3 format — shorter holes, lower intimidation for new golfers
  • Putting green for practice
  • Striped fairways — full course aesthetic
  • Multiple tee decks — different challenge levels
  • Pin and hole locations changed regularly — keeps regular play fresh
  • High standard of maintenance — professionally managed course

For many residents, walking 9 holes becomes a daily routine. The course also serves as the primary social gathering ground — walking rounds with neighbours is how connections are built here.

Security & Maintenance-Free Living

Two features consistently cited by Rosedale Village residents as their primary reasons for choosing the community:

24-Hour Gated Security

The community is fully enclosed with 24-hour automated gated security. During peak hours, a gatehouse concierge is on duty to manage guest entry. Residents access via automated gate system.

Professional Exterior Maintenance

All landscaping, lawn care, and exterior property upkeep is professionally managed. Residents describe this as truly maintenance-free: no mowing, no shoveling, no hiring contractors for exterior work. This is the primary practical reason downsizers choose Rosedale Village over a detached home in Credit Valley or Mount Pleasant — they retain ownership without the labour.

Community gardens are professionally maintained, but residents are welcome to participate in planting and landscape enhancement if they choose.

Who Moves to Rosedale Village

Rosedale Village draws a specific buyer profile:

  • Brampton family home sellers — Families who raised children in Castlemore, Credit Valley, or Northwest Brampton, now selling $1.2M–$2M+ homes and right-sizing. Rosedale keeps them in the city near adult children.
  • Active retirees — Buyers prioritizing daily golf, swimming, fitness classes, and active social programming over home maintenance
  • Semi-retirees (55–65) — Working part-time or newly retired, wanting resort-style amenities and community without moving to a retirement complex
  • Safety-priority buyers — 24-hour gated security is a decisive factor for many, particularly those who travel or whose spouses travel
  • South Asian retirees — Strong draw given Brampton's large Indian/Punjabi community — staying in the city near family while transitioning to maintenance-free life

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Rosedale Village Brampton?
Private gated adult lifestyle community (55+) in northwest Brampton at Sandalwood Parkway & Highway 410, built by Marycroft Homes. Centered around an executive par-3 9-hole golf course with full clubhouse: indoor pool, exercise room, saunas, billiards, fireplace lounge, tennis, lawn bowling, bocce, shuffleboard. What are home prices at Rosedale Village Brampton?
Wide range: condos from ~$299,900 (studio) to $589,900+ (2-bed, ~1,000 sq ft). Detached/bungalow resale up to $1,799,900. New Marycroft construction: $919,900–$1,255,900 (March 2026). Brampton's most complete 55+ price spectrum.
Who can live in Rosedale Village Brampton?
Age-restricted 55+ community. At least one resident per household must be 55+. Children cannot be permanent residents. Designed for retirees, semi-retirees, and empty nesters wanting maintenance-free, active, gated community living.
Is Rosedale Village maintenance-free?
Yes — professional landscaping, lawn care, and exterior maintenance are all managed by the community. Residents pay community fees that cover this. No mowing, no shoveling, no hiring exterior contractors. This is typically cited as the top reason buyers choose Rosedale Village over a comparable-priced standalone home.
What amenities does Rosedale Village have?
Indoor: heated pool, aquafit, exercise room, yoga/tai chi/zumba, saunas, billiards, cards, darts, fireplace lounge, library, hobby rooms, auditoriums, banquet facilities. Outdoor: tennis, lawn bowling, shuffleboard, bocce, chess garden, 2 parkettes. Golf: executive par-3 9-hole course with putting green. Social: dances, dinners, theme nights, hobby clubs.
Can you sell a Rosedale Village home?
Yes — Rosedale Village homes trade on the open market like any other property. Resale condos, bungalows, and detached homes are listed through MLS. The buyer must qualify under the 55+ age restriction. Contact Anu Kabli at (647) 200-5779 for current listings or to sell your Rosedale Village home.
