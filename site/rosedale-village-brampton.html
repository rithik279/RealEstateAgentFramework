About Rosedale Village

Rosedale Village is Brampton's premier 55+ adult lifestyle community, tucked into the northwest corner of the city near Heart Lake Road and Sandalwood Parkway. Unlike typical retirement developments, Rosedale Village offers the privacy of a gated neighbourhood combined with the convenience of being minutes from everything Brampton has to offer — Trinity Common Mall, Brampton Civic Hospital, Highway 410, and multiple GO bus corridors.

The community comprises approximately 600 bungalow and bungalow-townhome properties, predominantly semi-detached designs with one or two bedrooms, though some models offer loft spaces and finished basements. Homes were built primarily between the mid-1990s and early 2000s by Ballantry Homes and have been lovingly updated by their owners over the decades.

What sets Rosedale Village apart is its private residents-only recreation centre — The Village Centre — which includes an indoor swimming pool, fitness studio, tennis courts, bocce and lawn bowling greens, shuffleboard, billiards, a library, and a full schedule of social events and fitness classes. Monthly maintenance fees (approximately $250–$350/month) cover all common area maintenance, snow removal, and Village Centre access.

What to Know Before Buying in Rosedale Village

The 55+ Rule

At least one occupant per home must be 55 years of age or older. Spouses or partners under 55 can co-own and reside permanently. Grandchildren and family can visit but cannot be permanent residents. Buyers should confirm age eligibility before making an offer.

Monthly Maintenance Fees

Fees currently range from approximately $250–$350/month depending on the unit type. These cover the Village Centre operating costs, common area maintenance, and road clearing. When comparing Rosedale Village to a freehold home of similar price, factor in that these fees replace costs you'd otherwise pay individually (gym memberships, snow removal, landscaping).

Low Inventory — Act Fast

At any given time, only 5–15 homes in Rosedale Village are listed on MLS. When a well-priced home hits the market, it often has multiple showings within 48 hours. Working with an agent who tracks the community is essential — Anu monitors Rosedale Village listings and can alert you before new listings reach the broader public.

Financing Considerations

Lenders treat Rosedale Village properties like standard residential homes — not condos — because residents own their land and home. Standard mortgage qualification applies. However, some lenders will factor in the monthly maintenance fee when calculating debt service ratios, which can slightly affect maximum borrowing. Anu works with mortgage professionals who understand this community.
