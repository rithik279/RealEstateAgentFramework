About Northwood Park

Northwood Park is one of northwest Brampton's most established and sought-after family communities. Bounded by Bovaird Drive to the south, Sandalwood Parkway to the north, Heart Lake Road to the west, and Hurontario Street (Highway 10) to the east, the neighbourhood offers exceptional connectivity while maintaining a quiet, residential character that families return to again and again.

The housing stock spans several decades of Brampton's growth — from older 1980s two-storeys near the Bovaird corridor to newer executive detached homes closer to Sandalwood Parkway. This variety means Northwood Park can accommodate first-time buyers looking for a townhome entry point, young families seeking a semi-detached with good schools, and move-up buyers wanting a larger detached on a generous lot.

The neighbourhood is particularly popular with South Asian families, and has strong community anchors including Northwood Park itself (the greenspace), Loafers Lake Recreation Centre, and the Heart Lake Road commercial strip with grocery stores, restaurants, and services serving Brampton's multicultural community.

Schools in Northwood Park Brampton

Strong school catchments are one of the primary reasons families target Northwood Park. The neighbourhood is served by:

Secondary Schools

  • Sandalwood Heights Secondary School (PDSB) — One of Brampton's larger and well-regarded public high schools, located directly in the neighbourhood on Sandalwood Pkwy W. Strong arts and sports programs.
  • Cardinal Ambrozic Catholic Secondary School (DPCDSB) — Highly regarded Catholic high school on Castlemore Road. Strong academics and extracurriculars. Popular with South Asian Catholic families.
  • St. Marguerite d'Youville Secondary School — French-language Catholic option for families with French-language rights.

Elementary Schools

  • Northwood Public School — Within neighbourhood walking distance
  • St. Joachim Catholic School — Catholic elementary option
  • Earnscliffe Senior Public School — Grades 7–8 feeder to Sandalwood Heights

Why Families Choose Northwood Park

  • School catchment — Sandalwood Heights SS and Cardinal Ambrozic are consistently among parents' top choices in northwest Brampton
  • Highway 410 — On-ramp is 5 minutes away, making the commute to Mississauga, Toronto, or Vaughan manageable
  • Trinity Common Mall — Major shopping centre 10 minutes away with Walmart, LCBO, banks, restaurants, and big box retail
  • Heart Lake Conservation Area — Swimming, hiking, fishing. A true outdoor amenity 5 minutes from most Northwood Park addresses
  • Loafers Lake Recreation Centre — City facility with pool, gym, skating, and community programs
  • Multicultural community — Strong South Asian, Caribbean, and West African communities with corresponding restaurants, grocery stores (Nations Fresh Foods, Oceans Fresh Food Market), and places of worship
  • Established neighbourhood feel — Mature trees, wide streets, low turnover. People who move to Northwood Park tend to stay

Recent Market Conditions — Northwood Park

Northwood Park has maintained steady demand through Brampton's broader market fluctuations. Detached homes in good condition with updated kitchens and bathrooms continue to attract multiple offers, particularly in the $900K–$1.1M range. Townhomes in the $650K–$750K range appeal to first-time buyers who have been pushed out of Mississauga and downtown Toronto pricing.

Average days-on-market for well-priced Northwood Park properties runs 10–18 days, considerably lower than the broader Brampton average. Properties with basement apartments (legal or potential) command a premium as buyers increasingly factor in rental income potential.

Frequently Asked Questions

Where is Northwood Park in Brampton?
Northwood Park is in northwest Brampton, bounded by Bovaird Drive (south), Sandalwood Parkway (north), Heart Lake Road (west), and Hurontario Street/Hwy 10 (east). It's one of Brampton's most established family neighbourhoods with direct access to Highway 410.
What are home prices in Northwood Park Brampton?
Townhomes start around $650,000, semi-detached homes range from $780,000–$950,000, and detached homes sell from $900,000 to $1,350,000+ depending on size and upgrades.
What schools are in Northwood Park?
Northwood Park is served by Sandalwood Heights Secondary School (public), Cardinal Ambrozic Catholic Secondary School, Northwood Public School (elementary), and St. Joachim Catholic School. These are among the most sought-after school catchments in northwest Brampton.
Is Northwood Park safe?
Northwood Park is considered one of northwest Brampton's safer and more established neighbourhoods. It has a strong sense of community, low turnover, and active neighbourhood associations.
