## PLAIN ENGLISH GLOSSARY (Read This First)

### What is KD?
**KD = Keyword Difficulty** — Semrush's score from 0 to 100 measuring how hard it is to rank on Google page 1 for a keyword.

| KD Score | What it means | Time to rank (new site) |
|---|---|---|
| **0–14** | Easy — almost nobody competing | 2–8 weeks with a decent page |
| **15–29** | Easy-Medium — some competition | 2–4 months |
| **30–49** | Medium — established sites competing | 4–8 months + backlinks |
| **50–69** | Hard — need real DA + links | 6–12 months |
| **70+** | Very Hard — only big sites win | 12+ months |

Most of our target keywords are KD 10–35. This is the sweet spot — real search volume, achievable ranking.

### What is CPC?
**CPC = Cost Per Click** — what Google Ads advertisers pay per click on that keyword.
- Higher CPC = agents/brokers willing to pay more to appear = more competition = higher buyer intent
- CPC $15 (Milton) doesn't mean commissions are $15 — it means advertisers are BIDDING hard for those searchers because those leads convert well
- Lower volume + higher CPC = fewer but more serious, high-intent buyers (Milton buyers often pre-approved, serious)
- **User is correct:** CPC alone doesn't prove higher commission. What it proves = stronger buyer intent signal

### What is DA?
**DA = Domain Authority** (Moz score 0–100) — predicts ranking power based on backlinks.
- DA 1–20 = new/weak site
- DA 20–35 = mid-level (where most GTA agents sit)
- DA 35+ = established with real backlinks
