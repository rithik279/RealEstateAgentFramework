## SECTION 2: THE CONVERSION SYSTEM (How Traffic Becomes Leads)

### Why most agent sites don't convert

Average real estate agent website conversion rate: **0.5–1%**
Good agent website with proper conversion: **2–4%**
Agent with lead magnet + CRM follow-up: **4–8%**

Current Anu site estimate: ~0.5% (no form working, no value offer, no urgency)

**The 3 reasons traffic doesn't convert:**
1. No reason to submit contact info ("contact me" is not an offer)
2. No urgency or incentive
3. No immediate response when they do submit (people go cold in 5 minutes)

### The Conversion Stack (build in this order)

**Layer 1 — Fix What's Broken (This Week)**
- [ ] Fix Formspree (replace `REPLACE_WITH_FORM_ID` in index.html)
- [ ] Add phone number to nav bar
- [ ] Make phone number a tap-to-call link on mobile

**Layer 2 — Add Value Offers (Week 1–2)**
Replace "Contact Me" with specific offers:

| Offer | Headline | Who it attracts |
|---|---|---|
| Home Valuation | "Find Out What Your Brampton Home Is Worth — Free" | Sellers |
| Buyer Guide | "Download the Brampton First-Time Buyer Guide" | Buyers |
| Market Report | "Get the Latest Brampton Market Report (June 2026)" | Browsers → leads |
| Listings Alert | "See Homes Before They Hit MLS — Get Early Access" | Active buyers |

**Pick ONE primary offer per page.** Neighbourhood pages → "See homes in [neighbourhood]" offer. Agent pages → "Free Home Valuation" offer. Blog posts → Market report download.

**Layer 3 — Lead Capture Form (Every Page)**
Form fields that qualify WITHOUT scaring people off:
```
Name: [text field]
Phone: [tel field]  ← most important
Email: [email field]
I'm looking to: [Buy] [Sell] [Both] [Just browsing]
Timeline: [ASAP] [3-6 months] [6-12 months] [Just exploring]
```
5 fields max. More than 5 = people abandon.

**Layer 4 — CRM + Automated Follow-Up (Week 2–3)**
This is what Balwinder used. This is what William Nojavan meant by "40% conversation rate."
The key: **respond within 2 minutes or lose 80% of leads.**

Recommended free/cheap CRM options:
- **HubSpot Free** — CRM + email sequences + pipeline tracking, free forever
- **Go High Level** — $97/mo, purpose-built for real estate, includes SMS + email + landing pages
- **Mailchimp + Twilio** — DIY version, email automation + SMS, ~$30/mo combined

**Automated sequence after form submit:**
```
Minute 0:   Lead submits form
Minute 1:   Auto-SMS: "Hi [Name], this is Anu Kabli. Got your info re: [city] real estate.
            I'll call you shortly! In the meantime, here's today's listings: [link]"
Minute 2:   Auto-email: Thank you + relevant listings or market report PDF
Hour 1:     Personal call from Anu
Day 1:      Follow-up email with 3 matching properties
Day 3:      Market update email for their area
Day 7:      Check-in SMS: "Still looking for homes in [city]?"
Day 14:     Market report
Monthly:    Housing market update email
```
Leads who don't convert immediately need 6–12 touches. Most agents give up after 1–2.

**Layer 5 — Retargeting Pixel (Week 3)**
Install Facebook Pixel on every page. Anyone who visits but doesn't convert = show them Facebook/Instagram ads for $5–10/day.
Cost: ~$150–300/mo
Result: Visitors who saw the site 3–5x convert at 3x higher rate.
