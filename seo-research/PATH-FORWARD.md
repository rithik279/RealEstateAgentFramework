## WHAT GOOGLE REWARDS IN 2026 (Research Summary)

### The Algorithm Reality
1. **Topical Authority** — Google evaluates the *site*, not just the page. A site covering Brampton + Mississauga + Vaughan neighbourhood content ranks higher for each individual page than a site with one page on each.
2. **Information Gain** — Each page must add something portals can't produce. Sold price data + school Fraser scores + transit times + demographics = high gain. Generic listing grids = zero gain.
3. **Helpful Content System** — Now real-time, not periodic. Thin pages drag down the whole site. One weak page hurts ranking of all pages.
4. **Pillar → Cluster architecture** — City page links to neighbourhoods, neighbourhood pages link back. Blogs link to neighbourhood pages. Authority flows DOWN then back UP.
5. **E-E-A-T** — Experience, Expertise, Authoritativeness, Trust. Schema markup, author credentials, cited data sources, verified stats = higher trust score.
6. **Internal links in body** — Carry more weight than footer/nav links. Keyword-rich anchor text teaches Google what the linked page covers.

### Why Competitors Rank (From Real SERP Data)
- **byjesseandjoe.com** (ranks Lorne Park #3, Kleinburg #3, Port Credit #8) — Neighbourhood guides for 21 cities + live IDX listings (Repliers) + school/neighbourhood research tools
- **goodalemillerteam.com** (ranks Glen Abbey #4, Old Oakville #7, River Oaks #7) — Oakville-focused, neighbourhood content guides
- **dancooper.com** (Oakville, 2.1K/mo from 924 keywords) — 30 years of neighbourhood pages + 950 Google reviews + local guides like "Waterfront Parks of Oakville"
- **mcdadi.com** (Mississauga, 5.8K/mo but 62% is brand traffic) — Mostly name recognition, not SEO

### Portals Always Win City-Level (Accept This)
- Positions 1–5 for "homes for sale [city]" = realtor.ca, zolo.ca, housesigma.com, zoocasa.com, point2homes.com
- These have IDX data + massive DA. Anu cannot outrank them at city level.
- **Win condition: neighbourhood-level pages.** Portals generate listing grids, not local content. That gap is open.
