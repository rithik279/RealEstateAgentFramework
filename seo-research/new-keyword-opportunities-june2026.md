Nobody in Anu's network targets Ancaster, Stoney Creek, or Dundas. These are KD 14 keywords with 590–1,900/mo volume. Hamilton is 45min from Brampton. IQI Global covers all of Ontario — no reason to exclude it. Building 3 Hamilton pages could add 300–600/mo traffic in 6 weeks.

## CONTENT FORMULA FOR EACH PAGE

Every neighbourhood page needs to beat portals by having content portals can't auto-generate:

1. **Market stats table** — avg price by property type (detached/semi/town/condo), YoY change
2. **Neighbourhood character** — 2–3 paragraphs written with local knowledge, not generic
3. **Why buyers choose this neighbourhood** — specific draws (schools, transit, lifestyle)
4. **Top schools** — Fraser Institute scores, ranking out of all Ontario schools
5. **Transit section** — GO station/bus, commute time to downtown, highway access
6. **Compare to nearby areas** — table comparing price/lifestyle to 2 similar neighbourhoods
7. **5–6 FAQ items** — FAQPage JSON-LD schema
8. **Contact sidebar** — Formspree form (once configured)
9. **Explore More section** — links to 4 neighbouring areas (internal links)
10. **Breadcrumb** — links to city pillar page
11. **RealEstateAgent JSON-LD schema**

*Do NOT build thin pages. One solid 1,500-word page beats five 300-word pages.*
