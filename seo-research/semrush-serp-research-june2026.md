## THE BLOG STRATEGY — Why Portals Rank & How to Beat Them With Blogs

### What Portals Can't Do (Anu's Content Gap to Exploit)

Portals (realtor.ca, zolo.ca, zoocasa) rank for city-level keywords but produce ZERO of:
- Neighbourhood-specific sold price trend analysis
- School quality comparisons with data
- Transit + commute time breakdowns
- Lifestyle/community character descriptions
- "Is [neighbourhood] right for me?" buyer guides
- Market timing advice ("Should I buy in Kleinburg now?")

Google rewards pages that answer these questions. Portals can't because their content is auto-generated listing grids.

### Blog Formula Per Neighbourhood (5–6 posts each)

For each neighbourhood page, build these blog posts:

**Post 1: Market Overview**
- "[Neighbourhood] Housing Market Report — June 2026"
- Target: "[neighbourhood] housing market", "[neighbourhood] home prices 2026"
- What to include: avg sold price, days on market, sold-to-list ratio, month-over-month trend
- Volume: 20–50/mo per neighbourhood, very low KD

**Post 2: Neighbourhood Guide**
- "Living in [Neighbourhood] — Complete 2026 Guide"
- Target: "is [neighbourhood] a good place to live", "[neighbourhood] [city] review"
- What to include: schools, parks, transit, restaurants, demographics, community feel
- Volume: 30–100/mo, very low KD — portals don't have this content

**Post 3: School Guide**
- "Best Schools Near [Neighbourhood] [City]"
- Target: "[neighbourhood] schools", "schools in [neighbourhood]"
- What to include: EQAO scores, school types (French immersion etc.), catchment boundaries
- Note: Team Arora got 293/mo from "high schools in toronto" — replicate with hyperlocal versions

**Post 4: Buyer's Guide (Property Type Specific)**
- "Detached Homes in [Neighbourhood] — What to Expect in 2026"
- OR "Condos in [Neighbourhood] — Complete Buyer's Guide"
- Target: "detached homes [neighbourhood]", "townhouses [neighbourhood]"
- What to include: price ranges, common floorplans, age of homes, what to look for

**Post 5: Comparison Post**
- "[Neighbourhood] vs [Adjacent Neighbourhood] — Which is Better for Families?"
- Target: "[neighbourhood] vs [neighbourhood]", "best neighbourhood in [city] for families"
- Volume: Low individually but zero competition — captures decision-stage buyers

**Post 6: Investment/ROI Post**
- "Is [Neighbourhood] a Good Investment in 2026?"
- Target: "[neighbourhood] real estate investment", "homes appreciating [city]"
- What to include: 5-year price appreciation, rental yield, condo vs freehold ROI

## Unified Strategy From Research

### What ALL Top-Ranked Agent Pages Share (dancooper.com, mcdadi.com, teamarora.com)
1. **Neighbourhood specificity** — city-level pages rank for city terms, neighbourhood pages rank for neighbourhood terms
2. **Content depth** — minimum 800 words with real local data (schools, prices, transit)
3. **Schema markup** — RealEstateAgent + FAQ schema
4. **Internal links** — neighbourhood → city → agent page hierarchy
5. **Review velocity** — all top agents have 100+ Google reviews (Local Pack correlation)

### One Fixed Strategy for Every Neighbourhood Page
1. Build the main neighbourhood page (targeting primary keyword)
2. Publish Blog Post 1 (market report) within 2 weeks
3. Publish Blog Post 2 (neighbourhood guide) within 4 weeks
4. Publish Blog Post 3 (schools guide) within 6 weeks
5. Internal link: blog posts → neighbourhood page → city page → contact
6. Add FAQ schema to neighbourhood page
7. Submit to Search Console

**This is the playbook. Execute it neighbourhood by neighbourhood.**
