# RECO — Ontario Buyer Representation Obligations

## Registrant Obligations to Buyers (TRESA 2023)

Under the Trust in Real Estate Services Act (TRESA), Ontario registrants must:

1. Act in the best interests of their client
2. Maintain confidentiality of client information
3. Disclose all known material facts about a property
4. Avoid conflicts of interest or disclose them fully
5. Account for all money received on behalf of client
6. Provide conscientious and competent service

## Buyer Representation Agreement (BRA)

A BRA is a contract between the buyer and the brokerage. It establishes:
- Exclusive or non-exclusive representation
- Duration of the agreement
- Compensation structure
- Geographic scope
- Property type scope

Buyers are not legally required to sign a BRA, but registrants are required to present one.

Since December 2023 (TRESA Phase 2): written service agreement required before making or presenting an offer. Designated representation is now required.

## Material Latent Defects

Must be disclosed to buyers regardless of representation:
- Defects that render the property dangerous
- Defects that render the property unfit for habitation
- Defects that would not be discoverable through a reasonable inspection

Sellers are not required to disclose patent defects (visible to any reasonable person).

## Designated Representation

Under TRESA Phase 2 (December 2023):
- A registrant can represent both buyer and seller in different transactions as long as different registrants from the same brokerage handle each
- Self-represented parties (not clients) must be disclosed and treated honestly and fairly
- "Multiple Representation" (same registrant representing both buyer and seller) requires written consent from both parties

## FINTRAC Obligations

Registrants must verify identity of all buyers and sellers:
- Government-issued photo ID
- Secondary ID if high-risk transaction
- Politically Exposed Person (PEP) screening

Client identification records must be kept for 5 years.

## Privacy Obligations

PIPEDA and Ontario's MFIPPA apply:
- Collect only necessary personal information
- Use only for stated purposes
- Do not share without consent
- Maintain reasonable security safeguards

## Deposit Handling

Deposits must be:
- Held in the brokerage's trust account
- Not commingled with general funds
- Returned to buyer if conditions not met (with mutual release)
- Transferred to seller's lawyer on closing

## Important Disclaimers for AI-Generated Content

Any AI-generated information about properties, market conditions, legal matters, or financial advice must be reviewed by a licensed professional before being relied upon. AI tools assist with information gathering only — they do not replace the judgment of a licensed REALTOR or lawyer.

Registrants must review and approve all AI-generated communications before they are sent to clients.
