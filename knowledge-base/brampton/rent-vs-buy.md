# Rent vs Buy Analysis — Brampton 2025

## The Core Question

For someone renting in Brampton or GTA: at what point does buying make more financial sense than continuing to rent?

## Current Renting Costs in Brampton (2025)

- 1-bedroom apartment: $1,800 - $2,200/month
- 2-bedroom apartment: $2,200 - $2,800/month
- 3-bedroom townhouse: $2,800 - $3,400/month
- 3-bedroom detached basement: $2,200 - $2,800/month

## Buying Scenario: $850,000 Home

Assumptions: 10% down ($85,000), 5% rate, 25yr amortization.

- Monthly mortgage (with CMHC insurance): ~$4,700/month
- Property tax: ~$450/month
- Home insurance: ~$150/month
- Maintenance reserve: ~$300/month
- **Total monthly cost: ~$5,600/month**

With legal basement suite at $1,800/month:
- **Net monthly cost: ~$3,800/month**

This is comparable to or less than renting a 3-bedroom unit.

## Break-Even Timeline

Typically 3-5 years in Brampton given:
- Mortgage principal paydown (building equity)
- Property appreciation (Brampton avg 3-5% annually long-term)
- Rental income offset
- Rent is dead money; mortgage builds equity

## Rent vs Buy Decision Factors

Buying makes more sense when:
- Stable income and employment (2+ years same employer preferred)
- Plan to stay 3+ years
- Can manage upfront costs (down payment + closing costs ~3-4% of purchase)
- Rental costs are rising (Brampton rents up 8-15% since 2020)

Renting may be better when:
- Job or life situation likely to change within 2 years
- Cannot access minimum down payment
- Currently in stress test fail (need time to improve income/credit)

## The Hidden Cost of Waiting

On an $850,000 home appreciating at 4%/year:
- Year 1 wait = $34,000 in foregone appreciation
- Year 2 wait = $68,000 cumulative
- Plus rent paid during that period = $48,000-$68,000 (gone)

Total cost of waiting 2 years: approximately $116,000-$136,000 in value not captured.
