# Brampton Real Estate Market Overview

## City Profile

Brampton is located in Peel Region, Ontario, west of Toronto. Population ~650,000, one of Canada's fastest-growing cities. Strong South Asian community (largest in Canada per capita). Major employment: Pearson Airport corridor, logistics/warehousing, healthcare (Brampton Civic Hospital).

## Why Buyers Choose Brampton

1. **Value vs. Toronto**: Detached homes at $100-200k lower than comparable Toronto properties
2. **Basement income suites**: Legal basement apartments generate $1,200-$2,000/month — critical for affordability
3. **Family-oriented**: Large lots, good schools, community infrastructure
4. **Transit access**: GO Train (Brampton/Mount Pleasant stations), planned LRT (Hazel McCallion Line extension)
5. **Cultural community**: Strong South Asian, Caribbean, and Filipino communities

## Key Neighbourhoods

### Northwest Brampton (Credit Valley, Mount Pleasant)
- Newer builds (2010-present)
- Larger homes, newer infrastructure
- Popular with families upgrading
- Good transit (Mount Pleasant GO)

### Northwest (Heartlake, Sandringham)
- Mix of 1990s-2010 builds
- More affordable entry points
- High demand for basement income suites
- Community infrastructure mature

### Central/East (Bramalea, Brampton East)
- Older stock (1970s-1990s)
- Larger lots in some areas
- Price premium for fully renovated
- Strong South Asian community presence

## Basement Income Suite Market

Critical for Brampton buyers. Key facts:
- Legal basement apartments (separate entrance, proper ventilation, egress windows) rent for $1,400-$2,200/month
- Illegal suites carry risk: city can order closure, insurance complications, mortgage fraud risk
- Buyers should verify permits: Building permit for construction, Residential Rental Unit permit
- Many homes have "finished basements" without separate entrances — lower income potential

## ARU (Additional Residential Unit) Policy

City of Brampton permits up to 3 units on a residential property (Bill 23/Strong Mayors Act):
1. Main unit
2. Secondary suite (basement or main floor)  
3. Laneway/garden suite (where lot permits)

**Key requirement:** Each ARU must meet Ontario Building Code. Minimum bedroom size, ceiling height (6'5" minimum), egress windows required.

## Competition and Offer Strategy

Brampton market characteristics:
- **High demand**: First-time buyers + investors competing for same stock
- **Offer nights**: Common for desirable properties — list Tuesday, review offers Sunday
- **Conditional offers**: Less common in hot market; accepted in cooling periods
- **Deposits**: Typically 3-5% of purchase price within 24h of acceptance

## Schools (Key for South Asian Families)

Top-cited considerations:
- Peel District School Board (English public)
- Dufferin-Peel Catholic District School Board
- Private/Islamic schools in northwest Brampton
- School boundaries matter — specific streets can cross boundaries

## Red Flags for Brampton Buyers

1. Properties with unpermitted basement suites
2. Older (pre-2000) homes with knob-and-tube wiring — costly to remediate
3. Homes near industrial/logistics areas (noise, resale impact)
4. Properties with lead pipes (older stock, city testing program ongoing)
5. Boundary disputes on older lots
