# Brampton Real Estate Market Overview 2024-2025

## Market Conditions

Brampton is currently a buyer's market. Inventory has increased significantly from 2022 peaks. Buyers have more negotiating power than at any point in the last 5 years.

Average days on market (DOM) for detached homes: 25-40 days (up from 7-10 in 2022).
Average list-to-sale ratio: approximately 97-99%.

## Price Ranges (2025)

- Detached homes: $850,000 - $1,400,000
- Semi-detached: $750,000 - $950,000
- Townhouse (freehold): $650,000 - $850,000
- Condo townhouse: $550,000 - $700,000
- Condo apartment: $450,000 - $650,000

Most active price band for families: $750,000 - $1,100,000.

## Key Neighbourhoods

### Northwest Brampton (Sandringham-Wellington, Heart Lake)
Newer builds (2000s-2010s). Strong South Asian community. Close to Hwy 410. Popular with families upgrading from Toronto.

### Central Brampton (Queen/Main corridor)
Older stock (1970s-1990s). More affordable entry point. Transit accessible (GO, Zum BRT).

### Southeast Brampton (Bram East, Gore Rd)
Newer developments. Larger lots. Growing community.

### Southwest Brampton (Credit Valley, Mount Pleasant)
Near Mount Pleasant GO station. Good schools. Popular with commuters.

## What Brampton Buyers Prioritize

1. Legal basement suite / income potential — homes with separate entrance + legal suite command 5-10% premium. Rental income $1,500-$2,200/month offsets mortgage significantly.

2. Parking — minimum 3-4 spots (2 garage + 1-2 driveway). Multi-generational families often need 4-6.

3. Kitchen quality — open concept, updated kitchen. Frequently a deal-breaker.

4. Square footage — minimum 1,800-2,200 sqft for families. Multi-generational buyers want 2,500+.

5. Two-family setups — parents + adult children buying together. Budget pooling $1.2M-$1.6M combined is common.

## Affordability (2025)

5-year fixed rate: 4.5-5.2%. Stress test = contract rate + 2% (min 5.25%).

Income to qualify for $900K home (20% down, 25yr amortization, 5%): approx $160,000-$175,000 combined.

First-time buyer programs:
- Ontario LTT rebate: up to $4,000
- FHSA: up to $40,000 tax-free contribution
- Home Buyers Plan (RRSP): $35,000 per person ($70,000 per couple)
- Federal First-Time Home Buyer Tax Credit: $1,500
