# Basement Suites in Brampton — Income Potential Guide

## Why Basement Suites Matter in Brampton

Brampton has one of the highest rates of legal basement suite ownership in Ontario. For many buyers, the rental income from a basement suite is the deciding factor in affordability.

## Rental Income Estimates (2025)

- 1-bedroom basement suite: $1,400 - $1,800/month
- 2-bedroom basement suite: $1,800 - $2,400/month
- Bachelor/studio: $1,100 - $1,400/month

At $1,800/month rental income, that is $21,600/year. On a $950,000 home at 5% over 25 years, monthly mortgage payment is approximately $5,500-$5,800. Basement income offsets roughly 30-35% of the mortgage.

## Legal vs Illegal Basements

### Legal (registered second suite):
- Separate entrance
- Minimum ceiling height 6'5" (1.95m)
- Egress windows in all sleeping areas
- Separate laundry or shared arrangement
- Registered with City of Brampton
- Fire separation between units (Type X drywall)
- Carbon monoxide and smoke detectors in both units

### What makes it legal:
City of Brampton requires registration under the Second Suite By-Law. Registration costs ~$200-$500. Many homes have unregistered suites that can be legalized with minor upgrades.

## Garden Suites (Newer Opportunity)

As of 2023, Brampton allows garden suites (also called ADUs - Additional Dwelling Units) in rear yards on eligible lots.

Requirements:
- Minimum lot size: typically 300+ sqm rear yard
- Maximum 60% lot coverage after construction
- Maximum height: 6 metres (2 storeys)
- Must meet setback requirements (varies by zone)

Garden suites can generate $1,600-$2,200/month additional rental income. Build cost approximately $150,000-$250,000 for a 400-600 sqft unit.

## Key Questions to Ask About a Basement Suite

1. Is it registered with the city?
2. Does it have a separate entrance?
3. Are there egress windows?
4. How old are the electrical panel and plumbing?
5. Is there separate laundry, or shared?
6. What is the current rental rate if tenanted?

## Financing Note

When qualifying for a mortgage, lenders may count 50-100% of rental income depending on the lender. Many A-lenders will count 50% of current/market rental income to offset carrying costs.
