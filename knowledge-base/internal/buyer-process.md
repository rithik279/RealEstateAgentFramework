# Buyer Process Guide — Anu Kabli Real Estate

## Step 1: Initial Consultation (15-30 min)

Goals:
- Understand buyer's situation (renting/owning, timeline, area, budget)
- Identify key priorities (income suite, kitchen, parking, etc.)
- Assess financing status
- Set expectations on process and timeline

Output: Buyer profile, rough shortlist of suitable areas/types

## Step 2: Pre-Approval

If not pre-approved, refer to a mortgage broker immediately.
Pre-approval is critical — without it, we cannot write an offer.

Recommended: Connect buyer with mortgage broker within 24 hours of consultation.

Documents needed for pre-approval:
- 2 years T4/NOA (Notice of Assessment)
- 3 months bank statements
- Recent pay stubs
- Government ID
- Employment letter

## Step 3: Listing Search & Shortlisting

We set up MLS search alerts for buyer's criteria.
We also have access to pre-market/off-market listings through our network.

Shortlist: 5-10 homes meeting core criteria. Present to buyer.

## Step 4: Showings

Book 3-5 showings in a single day when possible (efficient + comparative).
After each showing: debrief with buyer on pros/cons.

Key things to note at showings:
- Condition of furnace, AC, roof, windows
- Basement: separate entrance? Ceiling height? Moisture?
- Kitchen: layout, condition, size
- Parking: count, garage size, driveway width
- Neighbourhood: traffic, parking, proximity to schools/transit

## Step 5: Offer Strategy

Comparable sales analysis (CMA) before any offer.
Discuss strategy: firm offer vs. conditional, price vs. conditions.

Standard conditions in buyer's market:
- Condition on financing (5 business days)
- Condition on home inspection (2-3 business days)
- Condition on status certificate (for condos, 3 business days)

In multiple offer situations:
- Certified deposit cheque ready (1-5% of purchase price)
- Pre-inspection if available
- Flexible closing date
- Clean offer if buyer can absorb risk

## Step 6: Accepted Offer → Closing

- Deposit due within 24 hours of acceptance (certified cheque or bank draft)
- Book lawyer immediately
- Book home inspection within 2-3 business days
- Insurance binder needed before closing
- Final walkthrough 24 hours before closing
- Closing costs: 1.5-4% of purchase price (LTT, legal fees, adjustments)

## Timeline: Typical Purchase

- Week 1-2: Consultation + pre-approval
- Week 2-6: Showings
- Week 6-8: Offer + accepted
- Week 8-12: Condition removal + closing preparation
- Closing day: Keys received

Total typical timeline from first call to keys: 8-16 weeks.
