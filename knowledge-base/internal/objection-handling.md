# Objection Handling Scripts — Buyer Calls

## "I'm just browsing / not ready yet"

Response: "Totally fair, no pressure at all. A lot of people say that and then end up buying within 3-6 months when they find the right place. Can I ask — is there something specific holding you back? Sometimes it's something we can actually solve."

Follow with: What would need to change for you to feel ready?

## "The market is going to drop, I'm waiting"

Response: "I hear that a lot. Here's what I can tell you — we've been in a buyer's market for about 18 months now in Brampton. Prices are down from the 2022 peak. The question isn't whether prices will drop more — it's whether the cost of waiting (rent + missed equity) outweighs a potential 5% price decline. Want me to run those numbers for you?"

Key point: Timing the market is extremely difficult. Most people who waited in 2020 "for prices to drop" missed 40% appreciation.

## "I need to sell my house first"

Response: "That's a very common situation. There are a few strategies — conditional purchase, bridge financing, or listing first. What's your timeline on selling? Let's map out a plan so you're not caught in a gap."

## "I'm already working with another agent"

Response: "Got it. Have you signed a buyer representation agreement with them? If not, you're not committed. And even if you are, it's worth knowing what options are out there. We work a bit differently — we're very focused on finding the right home vs. just closing a deal. No pressure."

## "I need to think about it / talk to my spouse"

Response: "Of course, that makes complete sense. What would be most helpful — should I send you some information first so you both have something to look at together? Or would it be easier if I jumped on a quick call with both of you?"

## "Interest rates are too high"

Response: "Rates are higher than 2021, for sure. But here's the thing — variable rates have come down from their peak, and most economists expect further cuts in 2025. You can always refinance when rates drop. But you can't go back and buy at today's prices once they move up. Want me to show you what the numbers look like at different rate scenarios?"

## "I can't afford it / I don't have the down payment"

Response: "How much do you currently have saved? Sometimes there are programs that can help — FHSA, Home Buyers Plan, gifted down payments. Let's see what you actually need vs. what you have. You might be closer than you think."

## "I found a place online I like, can you help me see it?"

Response: "Absolutely. I can set up a showing today or tomorrow. What's the address? I'll pull the full listing details and we can talk about what to look for when we're there."

## "Why should I use you vs. another agent?"

Response: "Fair question. We specialize specifically in Brampton and the GTA. We have access to listings before they hit the public market. And we're honest — if a home isn't right for you, we'll tell you. Our goal is to find you the right home, not just close a deal. Want to see what we've done for buyers in your price range?"
