# Buyer Consultation Script — Brampton/GTA Market

## Purpose
Script for the initial buyer consultation call (15-30 min). Run this after the AI pre-qualification call identifies the buyer as warm (50-79) or hot (80+).

## Before the Call

Review the buyer's extracted profile:
- Readiness score and tier
- Budget range and pre-approval status
- Specific property interest
- Key signals: basement income, parking, family size, timeline

---

## Opening (2 min)

"Hi [Name], it's [Your Name] from [Brokerage]. Thanks for connecting — I saw you were looking at [Brampton/area] homes in the [budget] range. I've got about 15 minutes — is now still a good time?"

**If they mention a specific property:**
"Great — I actually pulled that listing before our call. Let's talk about it."

**If no specific property:**
"I want to understand exactly what you're looking for so I can find you the right options — not just show you everything on the market."

---

## Qualifying Questions (5-8 min)

### 1. Timeline
"When are you looking to be in your new home?"
- ASAP → hot sequence
- 3 months → warm sequence  
- 6+ months → early nurture

### 2. Financing
"Have you spoken with a mortgage broker yet? Do you have a pre-approval?"
- Pre-approved → confirm amount, get letter
- Not started → offer mortgage referral (see referral SOP)
- Working on it → book follow-up

**Note:** Under TRESA, you must recommend they seek independent mortgage advice. Never steer toward a specific lender without disclosure.

### 3. Representation
"Are you currently working with another agent?"
- Yes → "I respect that. If things don't work out, keep me in mind."
- No → explain BRA (Buyer Representation Agreement) process

### 4. Must-Haves (Brampton-specific)
"Walk me through your ideal home — what are the non-negotiables?"

Probe for:
- Basement income suite (separate entrance, legal suite)
- Parking (number of spots, garage vs. surface)
- Kitchen quality (open concept, updated)
- Bedrooms for extended family
- School boundaries (important for South Asian families)
- Transit/commute to Toronto

### 5. Budget Reality Check
"The $X range you mentioned — is that the total purchase price, or your monthly payment comfort?"

Brampton detached market reference:
- $600k-700k → townhouses, some semis (check latest market)
- $700k-850k → detached entry-level, semis
- $850k+ → detached with more land/upgrades

---

## BRA Discussion (3-5 min)

"Before I can formally represent you, Ontario law requires us to sign a Buyer Representation Agreement. This protects you — it means I'm legally working for you, not the seller."

Key points to communicate:
- BRA is required under TRESA before we can share confidential strategies
- You choose the duration (recommend 90 days)
- It's not a lock-in — if you're unhappy, we discuss options
- What it means for you: fiduciary duty, confidentiality, undivided loyalty

**Do NOT pressure or incentivize signing BRA. This is an ethical and legal requirement.**

---

## Setting Expectations (2 min)

"Here's how I work:
1. I'll send you a shortlist within 24 hours based on what you told me
2. Each listing comes with a Home Fit Report — scored on your specific priorities
3. If you see something you like, we move fast. Good Brampton homes get offers within 7 days
4. I'll coach you on offer strategy before we submit anything"

---

## Close (1-2 min)

**Hot/Warm:**
"I want to get you a shortlist today. Can I send it to [email/text] this afternoon?"

**Early:**
"Let me put together an affordability breakdown for your budget in Brampton — I'll send it over by tomorrow. Sound good?"

---

## After the Call

- [ ] Send Home Fit Report for top 3 matching listings (if MLS connected)
- [ ] Send affordability guide PDF if first-time buyer
- [ ] Log notes to lead profile
- [ ] Schedule BRA signing if warm/hot
- [ ] Add to appropriate nurture sequence

---

## Common Objections

**"I'm just looking"**
"That's perfectly fine — a lot of buyers start that way. The market moves fast, so even if you're 3-6 months out, knowing what's available helps you make a better decision when you're ready. Mind if I send you 3 listings that fit what you described?"

**"I need to sell my current home first"**
"We can help with that too. The key is knowing your net proceeds before you make offers. Would it help if I pulled some comparable sales in your area so you know what your home is worth?"

**"My cousin is an agent"**
"That's great! If they're in the Brampton market and you're comfortable working with them, absolutely do that. I just want to make sure you have the right representation for this purchase. Is your cousin doing a lot of Brampton detached homes right now?"

**"I want to think about it"**
"Of course. What would you need to know to feel confident moving forward? I want to make sure I've given you everything you need."
